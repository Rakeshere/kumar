KRAFT homepage

Open index.html in a browser. Keep the folders together.

index.html      page structure and all text
css/style.css   fonts, positions, colors, hover states
script.js       page scaling and heart toggle
assets/         background art and photos taken from the design image
fonts/          closest free fonts (Libre Caslon Display, Newsreader, Marcellus, Inter; SIL Open Font License)
vectors/        SVG icons and logo (redrawn) and SVG traces of the ornaments (approximate)

Known gaps: fonts are lookalikes, artwork is limited to the 1024 px source, 
phone view is a scaled desktop view, menus, search, slider and forms have no backend or content.
